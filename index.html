⚡ At a Glance

💰 Blocked Account – Usually NOT needed

If your net training wage is ≥ €822/month, no blocked account is required. Only for wages below that or unpaid school‑based programs.

Fallback: €11,904/year

🗣️ German Level – B1 minimum

B1 required for most fields; B2 for nursing/healthcare. A2 may be accepted in some technical trades, but riskier.

⏱️ A1 → B1 typically takes 8–14 months.

⏳ Realistic Timeline

From zero German to arrival: 9–18 months.
Embassy appointments: 3–6 months wait.
IBCC/HEC attestation: 4–8 weeks – start early!

🛡️ Fraud Prevention

Never pay large upfront “placement” fees.
Verify employer is registered with IHK/HWK.
Cross‑check listings on arbeitsagentur.de.
Legitimate Ausbildung pays you – no tuition.

📋 Key Requirements & Costs

✅ Eligibility: Age 16+ (over 35 may face extra questions), Matric minimum, clean police record, genuine signed offer.

📄 Documents: Valid passport, signed contract, Chamber registration, B1/B2 certificate, attested educational docs, German CV, motivation letter, proof of finances, health insurance, accommodation proof, biometric photos.

⏱️ Realistic Timeline (from zero to arrival)

Months 1–10 – German to B1 Months 6–10 – Job search Months 10–12 – Contract & attestation Months 12–17 – Embassy & processing Month 18+ – Arrival & start
🔥 Highest-demand fields for international candidates (2026): Nursing/geriatric care, IT specialist, electronics/mechatronics, logistics (warehouse & freight forwarding), skilled trades (electrician, plumber, HVAC).
📜 Legal basis: §16a AufenthG (training visa) – requires signed contract.
🔑 §17 AufenthG – “Ausbildungsplatzsuche” (9‑month search visa) if you don’t have a contract yet.
🛂 Permanent residence: Facilitated path via §18c after qualified employment. Citizenship after 5 years (dual allowed with Pakistan).
