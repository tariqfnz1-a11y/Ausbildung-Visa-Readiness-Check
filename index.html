    FAQ

    Common questions from Pakistani applicants

    Short, direct answers. Always reconfirm specifics with the embassy or VFS before acting on them.

    Do I need a job offer before I can apply for the visa?

    Yes, for the standard paid Ausbildung visa (§16a AufenthG) you need a signed training contract first. If you don't have one yet, a separate visa — the §17 AufenthG "Ausbildungsplatzsuche" (training-place search) visa — lets you enter Germany for up to 9 months on a single entry specifically to find and sign one. It has its own, separate requirements.

    What's the actual minimum German level — can I get away with less than B1?

    B1 is the practical minimum for most trades, and B2 for healthcare fields (nursing, dental, pharmacy) and bank clerk roles. A few technical/industrial employers will accept A2 with a commitment to reach B1 during training, but this is employer-specific and carries more interview risk — don't count on it as your plan.

    Do I need a blocked account (Sperrkonto) if my apprenticeship is paid?

    Usually not. If your training wage is at least around €822 net per month (2026 figure), your contract and payslip are normally accepted as proof of financial means on their own. A blocked account (~€11,904/year) is mainly needed if your wage is lower than that, or if the training is unpaid/school-based.

    How much does this whole process cost in total?

    Excluding flights and any blocked account, realistically PKR 350,000–750,000 (~$1,250–$2,700), most of it going to German language courses and exam fees rather than the visa itself. The national visa fee is €75 and the VFS service fee is €38 (2026 baseline).

    How long does the whole process actually take?

    From zero German to arrival in Germany, 9–18 months is realistic. The two biggest levers are how long it takes you to reach B1/B2, and the current 3–6 month embassy appointment and processing wait from Pakistan.

    Is there an age limit?

    Generally 16 and up, with no strict legal upper limit. Applicants over roughly 35 may face more questions at the visa interview about their motivation, but older applicants are not automatically refused.

    Will my Pakistani Matric/FSc certificates be accepted?

    Generally yes, once attested through IBCC (school certificates) or HEC (degrees) and legalized as the embassy requires. Recognition is still assessed case by case for the specific training program, so start attestation early — it's usually the slowest single step (4–8 weeks).

    Can I bring my spouse or children on this visa?

    Generally not in the early stages of a training-visa category — this route is designed around the individual trainee. Family reunification rules are separate and stricter for trainees than for established skilled workers.

    Can I switch employers if I don't like the one I signed with?

    Your residence permit is tied to the specific training contract you entered on. Changing employers mid-training is possible but requires approval from the local Foreigners' Office (Ausländerbehörde) — it's not automatic.

    What happens after I finish the Ausbildung — can I stay in Germany?

    Yes. Qualified professionals commonly earn €2,000–3,500/month after completion. A facilitated path to permanent residence exists under §18c AufenthG after a few years of qualified employment, and citizenship is possible after 5 years of legal residence under the 2024 reform — which also allows dual nationality, relevant since Pakistan recognizes dual citizenship with Germany.

    Is a driving license necessary?

    Only for specific trades. A professional/truck driver apprenticeship (Berufskraftfahrer) usually expects you to already hold a Class B (car) license before starting, and you train toward Class C/CE during the program. For most other trades a driving license isn't required, though it's a plus for logistics and automotive roles.

    How do I avoid fake "guaranteed placement" agents?

    Never pay a large upfront fee for a "guaranteed" apprenticeship. Verify any employer independently through the relevant German Chamber (IHK/HWK) registry, and cross-check the listing on the official arbeitsagentur.de job board. A genuine Ausbildung pays you — be suspicious of any program asking you to pay tuition for one.

    Does this calculator submit my information anywhere?

    No. Everything runs locally in your browser — nothing is stored, logged, or sent to a server. You can fill it in as many times as you like as your situation changes.
